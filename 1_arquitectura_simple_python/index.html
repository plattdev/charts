About This Visualization & Methodology

Core Question: Which countries are highly efficient at converting their economic resources into actual human well-being and sustainability, and which wealthy nations are underperforming?

1. X-Axis: Sustainable Development (Overall Score)

Normalized score (0–100) measuring each country's overall achievement across all 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), serving as the headline metric of the Sustainable Development Report.

2. Y-Axis: World Happiness Index (3-Year Average)

National life evaluation score (0–10) averaged over the three preceding survey years (e.g., 2022–2024). Using a 3-year rolling average provides a larger sample size for more stable and precise estimates.

3. Bubble Size & Color: GDP per Capita, PPP (Constant 2021 $)

Following the official World Happiness Report statistical appendix, wealth is measured via Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) at constant 2021 international dollars (using the latest available year). PPP accounts for local cost-of-living differences, while constant prices remove inflation distortion. Bubble radius scales with √GDP so visual area accurately reflects wealth volume.
