Which countries convert their economic resources into actual human well-being and sustainability?

Comparing 140 nations across Sustainable Development (SDI), World Happiness (WHR), and GDP per capita (PPP).

About This Visualization & Methodology

Core Question: Which countries are highly efficient at converting their economic resources into actual human well-being and sustainability, and which wealthy nations are underperforming?

1. X-Axis: Sustainable Development (Overall Score)

Normalized score (0–100) measuring each country's total achievement across all 17 UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), serving as the headline metric of the Sustainable Development Report.

2. Y-Axis: World Happiness Index (3-Year Average)

National life evaluation score (0–10) averaged over the three preceding survey years (e.g., 2022–2024). Ranking countries by their 3-year rolling average provides a larger combined sample size for more precise estimates.

3. Bubble Area & Color: GDP per Capita, PPP (Constant 2021 $)

Aligned with the World Happiness Report statistical appendix, wealth is measured via Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) at constant 2021 international dollars (latest available year). PPP adjusts for local cost of living, constant prices eliminate inflation distortion, and scaling radius by √GDP ensures bubble area reflects wealth volume.
